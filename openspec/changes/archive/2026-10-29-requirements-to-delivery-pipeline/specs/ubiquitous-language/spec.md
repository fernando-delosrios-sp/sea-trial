## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Verification Agent term

The glossary SHALL define **Verification Agent** as the agent responsible for validating deliverable outcomes, prompting the Tech Specialist for proof links and configuration notes when a deliverable reaches `Validation required` or `Accepted`, and writing the verified proof back into the Requirements Canvas.

#### Scenario: Verification Agent term in glossary

- **GIVEN** this change archives
- **WHEN** the ubiquitous-language spec is updated
- **THEN** a Verification Agent term entry SHALL exist with definition, context, and aliases

### Requirement: Delivery Proof & Outcome term

The glossary SHALL define **Delivery Proof & Outcome** as the structured markdown block embedded under each requirement heading in the Requirements Canvas documenting customer outcome summary, visual proof links (e.g. Loom, demo recordings), SailPoint and external components, and configuration artefacts.

#### Scenario: Delivery Proof & Outcome term in glossary

- **GIVEN** this change archives
- **WHEN** the ubiquitous-language spec is updated
- **THEN** a Delivery Proof & Outcome term entry SHALL exist with definition, context, and aliases

### Requirement: Plan Deliverables Action Link term

The glossary SHALL define **Plan Deliverables Action Link** as the workflow link trigger embedded in the Requirements Canvas header (`[🚀 Plan Deliverables]({{planDeliverablesUrl}})`) that triggers the Deliverables Agent to initiate scoping and create items in the Deliverables List.
